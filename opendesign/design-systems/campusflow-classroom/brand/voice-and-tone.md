# Voice and tone

## Voice

CampusFlow sounds like a concise editorial note: observant, direct, and lightly personal. It does not describe a community, forum, or startup mission.

## UI copy

- Use short nouns for navigation: Today, Profile, Account.
- Use clear verbs for actions: Search, Save, Edit, Connect, Print.
- Let visual headings carry mood; keep supporting copy practical.
- Technical proof may name OAuth, JWT, REST, and providers when useful for the class demo.

## Examples

- Preferred: “A quiet Thursday in Kyoto.”
- Preferred: “Clouds thinning after three.”
- Preferred: “Connected with GitHub.”
- Avoid: “Your ultimate international student community.”
- Avoid: “Empowering global campus connections.”

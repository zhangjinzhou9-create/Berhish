# Style notes

The manga influence is expressed through original web-design principles rather than copied characters or panels:

- abrupt shifts between close crop and wide negative space;
- a mundane daily subject treated with cinematic attention;
- black rules and registration marks that suggest printed pages;
- off-axis captions and chapter numbering;
- moments of vermilion used like editorial marks.

Torn edges use restrained polygon masks. They should feel cut by hand without becoming scrapbook decoration. The main photograph remains legible and the interface stays calm enough for projection.
